## Equipment Options

**Presidential glass** — two transparent panels flanking a podium. The speaker sees the script; the audience sees through the glass. Used for keynotes, political addresses, commencement speeches and galas.

**Camera-mount prompter** — text sits in front of the camera lens so the talent reads while looking directly at the viewer. Used for corporate videos, commercials, training content and broadcast segments.

**Floor and confidence monitors** — screens at stage level for presenters who want a safety net without a full prompter rig. Common at panel discussions, awards shows and multi-speaker conferences.

## Why an Operator Matters

The hardware is straightforward. The skill is in the person running it — matching scroll speed to a speaker who speeds up under nerves, pausing when they go off-script, pulling in a revised paragraph minutes before a session starts. That is what you are really booking when you hire a teleprompter service.

Our NYC-area operators work from a bench of professionals who know the city's venues and production rhythms. They arrive early, coordinate with your crew and stay focused on the talent from rehearsal through wrap.
