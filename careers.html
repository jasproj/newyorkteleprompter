Who We're Looking For

New York Teleprompter is part of the National Teleprompter network — 25+ years, 1,000+ shows, one standard.   • Real show experience — you've run scripts live with an executive or a politician at the podium
  • Reliable transportation and the ability to be on site early, every time
  • A professional manner with clients, AV crews and talent

What You Get

  • Competitive day rates, paid per show — no chasing clients for money

  • Repeat work with corporate and production-company clients who come back year after year
  • A national network behind you for multi-city and travel shows
